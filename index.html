Welcome / Καλώς Ήρθατε

Welcome to the official portal of the Greek Orthodox Church of the Archangels in George. Located in the heart of the Garden Route, we are a vibrant parish connecting generations of Greek families, youth, and Orthodox faithful through liturgy, fellowship, and tradition.
